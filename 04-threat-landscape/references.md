# References and Further Reading

This document records authoritative resources used to support the Threat Landscape module. Definitions should be interpreted in the context of the relevant publication.

## 1. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)

### Threat

NIST Cybersecurity and Information Security Resource Center — Threat Glossary

https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/threat

Use this resource to examine definitions of threats and their potential effects on systems and organizations.

### Vulnerability

NIST Cybersecurity and Information Security Resource Center — Vulnerability Glossary

https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/vulnerability

Use this resource to understand vulnerabilities as weaknesses in information systems, procedures, controls, or implementation.

### Risk

NIST Cybersecurity and Information Security Resource Center — Risk Glossary

https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/risk

Use this resource to study risk in relation to potential adverse impacts and the likelihood of occurrence.

### Risk Assessment

NIST Special Publication 800-30 Revision 1 — Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments

https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final

This publication provides guidance for assessing information security risks.

## 2. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)

### Phishing-Resistant Multifactor Authentication

CISA — Implementing Phishing-Resistant MFA

https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-01/fact-sheet-implementing-phishing-resistant-mfa-508c.pdf

This guidance explains how MFA can protect accounts and why phishing-resistant methods offer stronger protection against credential theft.

### Using the References

- Consult primary sources when validating security terminology.
- Distinguish formal definitions from simplified educational explanations.
- Check the publication and revision context when citing a standard or guideline.
- Do not treat a general reference as proof that a particular security incident occurred.
