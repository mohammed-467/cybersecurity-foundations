\# The CIA Triad



The CIA Triad is a core cybersecurity model used to guide the protection of information and systems.



CIA stands for:



\- \*\*Confidentiality\*\*

\- \*\*Integrity\*\*

\- \*\*Availability\*\*



\## 1. Confidentiality



Confidentiality means information is accessible only to authorized people, systems, or processes.



\### Example



A student's grades should be visible to the student, authorized teachers, and school administrators — not to everyone.



\### Controls that support confidentiality



\- Strong passwords

\- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

\- Access controls

\- Encryption

\- Least privilege



\## 2. Integrity



Integrity means data remains accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized changes.



\### Example



A bank transfer amount must not be changed from `$100` to `$1,000` by an attacker.



\### Controls that support integrity



\- File hashes

\- Digital signatures

\- Access controls

\- Input validation

\- Audit logs



\## 3. Availability



Availability means authorized users can access systems and data when they need them.



\### Example



A hospital system must remain available so medical staff can access patient information during an emergency.



\### Controls that support availability



\- Backups

\- Redundant systems

\- Disaster recovery plans

\- System monitoring

\- Protection against denial-of-service attacks



\## CIA Triad summary



| Principle | Main question | Example |

|---|---|---|

| Confidentiality | Who is allowed to see the information? | Only authorized staff can view employee records. |

| Integrity | Is the information accurate and unchanged? | A file hash detects whether a file was altered. |

| Availability | Can authorized users access the information? | Backups help restore data after a failure. |



\## What I learned



The CIA Triad helps cybersecurity professionals evaluate risk and choose suitable controls. A strong security program protects confidentiality, integrity, and availability together.
